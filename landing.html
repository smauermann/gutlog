Questions

Good to know

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Gutlog has no server and no account. Everything you log is stored only in your browser, on your device. We literally cannot see it — and there's nothing to be breached or sold.

Does it really work offline?

Yes. It's a single page that runs entirely in your browser. Add it to your home screen and it works with no signal at all. Tip: open it once with a connection, then it's available anytime.

Is this a subscription?

No. You pay €5 once and the app is yours. No recurring charges, no account to cancel, no feature locked behind a paywall later.

Will it tell me which foods are safe to eat?

No — and be wary of anything that claims to. Gutlog highlights foods that tend to show up before your flares so you have concrete patterns to test with your dietitian or GI. It's a memory aid, not a diagnosis.

How do I keep my data if I switch phones?

One tap exports a backup file (and a CSV). Restore it on the new device in seconds. Because nothing is in the cloud, you're always in control of your own copy.

What exactly do I get for €5?

A single file you open in any modern browser and add to your home screen — the complete app, with free updates. No install, no app-store account.
